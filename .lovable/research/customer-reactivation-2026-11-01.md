Both keyword_compare and keyword_research returned 30/month and KD 0/100 for the chosen exact keyword, customer reactivation. This is niche demand, not a guarantee of ranking or a measured visitor count. KYC abandonment, KYC drop off and whatsapp customer retention had missing metrics, not verified zero demand. The exact-keyword SERP tool returned no data; organic ranking positions and ranking difficulty beyond the supplied estimate remain unknown. Question suggestions were noisy and mostly zero-volume, so none was used as a separate validated target.

Chosen angle: diagnose inactivity against purchase behaviour before a B2B win-back campaign. More tightly scoped and better supported by the portfolio than a broad retention-strategies guide.
