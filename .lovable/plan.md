## The positioning
**One line:** "Retention and lifecycle marketer for repeat-purchase businesses. I find why customers stop coming back, then fix it with CRM journeys."

Why this works:
- Your real proof is in this area: ApnaKlub repeat orders, the 5–7% order-rate lift, the 20% CRM cost cut, and ₹8L/month saved.
- Your articles already cover it: retention rate, win-back campaigns, CRM vs product.
- "Growth marketer" is crowded. "Retention for repeat-purchase businesses" (fintech, B2B commerce, marketplaces) is a narrower space you can own.
